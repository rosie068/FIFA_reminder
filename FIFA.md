Group composition is confirmed: the **Final Draw was held 12:00 EST, December 5, 2025 at the John F. Kennedy Center, Washington, D.C.** The tournament runs June 11 – July 19, 2026; the **Final is at New York New Jersey Stadium (MetLife, East Rutherford) on July 19**.

**Title market (FOX Sports / FanDuel, June 12, 2026):** Spain +450, France +500, England +700, Portugal +800, Brazil +900, Argentina +950 — the only six teams the market treats as genuine contenders, with a clear drop to Germany (~+1300) and Netherlands (~+1600).

**Methodology for predictions:** For each match, `bookmaker_odds` are real 3-way moneylines (American format) aggregated from oddschecker, FanDuel and DraftKings snapshots dated June 12, 2026. The `probabilities` are derived by converting the three moneylines to implied probabilities, removing the bookmaker margin ("de-vigging") to make them sum to ~1.0, then nudging for known injuries/suspensions (e.g., South Africa's two suspensions, Spain's managed Yamal minutes, Brazil's missing Rodrygo/Estêvão). `predicted_outcome` is the highest-probability result; `confidence` is high (favorite implied >60%), medium (45–60%), or low (near-even/three-way coin-flips).

**Structural notes for the consuming app:**
- Three host nations play all group games at home: Mexico (Group A — Mexico City, Guadalajara, Monterrey), Canada (Group B — Toronto, Vancouver), USA (Group D — Los Angeles, Seattle, Santa Clara).
- FIFA renamed venues for the tournament (e.g., MetLife = "New York New Jersey Stadium", SoFi = "Los Angeles Stadium", AT&T = "Dallas Stadium"); the JSON uses the real stadium names with host city for clarity.
- After Matchday 1, the market collapsed advancement odds for elite teams to roughly −10000 (Spain, Argentina, Brazil, England, Germany, Mexico) — useful context for any "to advance" market the app adds later.

## Recommendations
- **Scheduler/reminders:** Consume the `matches` array directly; every `kickoff_pst` is already PDT (UTC−7). Build reminders off `date` + `kickoff_pst`. Use `status` ("completed" / "in_progress" / "scheduled") to drive notification logic and hide finished games. Re-poll daily — three matches are already final and one is live.
- **Predictor:** Treat `bookmaker_odds` as ground truth and `probabilities` as the de-vigged estimate. The biggest edges for a bettor sit in the "low confidence" near-even games (matches 10, 23, 43, 49, 59, 69, 71) and the toughest-group decider Norway–Senegal (43) — these are where lineup/rotation news moves lines most. **Refresh odds within 24 hours of each kickoff**, because favorites that have already qualified (e.g., France vs Norway, match 61) often rotate and shift prices materially.
- **Thresholds that should change a pick:** a confirmed starting-XI rotation by a qualified favorite (downgrade their win probability ~10–15 pts toward a draw), a new injury/suspension to a listed key player, or a line move of >20 cents on the favorite's moneyline (treat as sharp money and re-weight `probabilities` toward the new market).
- **Data hygiene:** After each match, write the final score into `result` and flip `status` to "completed"; recompute downstream group-stage standings and the eight-best-third-place race (FIFA tiebreakers: points, goal difference, goals scored, then head-to-head).

## Caveats
- **USA vs Paraguay (match 4)** was in progress at publication time (June 12, 9pm ET kickoff); its `result` is null — update post-match.
- **Odds are point-in-time snapshots** (June 12, 2026) and will drift; lower-profile fixtures' lines are partly model-derived where books had not posted firm prices, and individual sportsbooks differ (e.g., FanDuel had Switzerland −475 vs the −425 aggregate on Qatar–Switzerland).
- **Probabilities are estimates**, not guarantees; the expanded 48-team format (8 of 12 third-placed teams advance) makes group-stage incentives unusual — favorites may rest players in dead-rubber or already-qualified final-round games, which the static odds may not yet reflect.
- **Manager note:** Saudi Arabia changed its head coach roughly eight weeks before the tournament; the JSON flags this rather than asserting a confirmed name.
- A handful of `kickoff_pst` values for the simultaneous final-round group games (e.g., the two Group B and two Group A finales) reflect the same slot in different venues/time zones, all normalized to PDT.
